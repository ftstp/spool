Recorded instruments (piano, strings by section, pizzicato, harp, woodwinds, brass, timpani,

Upright Piano sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio. Credit to the original authors and
Versilian Studios, encouraged by the library, is given here with thanks.

Each note was trimmed, faded, normalised, made mono and encoded as Opus by
tools/make_instruments.py and tools/make_orchestra.py (a held instrument's notes with a
sustain loop crossfaded into them).
